## 👋 About Us

**ANA Digital Media** is a ROI-focused digital marketing agency based in Mumbai, India. We help businesses generate real leads and sales through data-driven campaigns, with full transparency in strategy, budget, and reporting.

This GitHub organization is home to our engineering team: the people who build the websites, landing pages, tools, and internal platforms that power our campaigns and our clients' growth.

## 🤝 Engineering Principles

- **Results over guesswork.** We build what's measurable and ship what moves the numbers.
- **Keep it simple.** Clear, maintainable code that fits existing patterns beats clever abstractions.
- **Transparency.** Decisions are documented, and work is easy to review and trace.
- **Quality by default.** Performance, accessibility, and security are part of the job, not extras.
